# Telling your manager

For the "you asked me to use AI, here is what I did" conversation. Say it in under a minute. Numbers before adjectives.

## The 3-line script (spoken)

1. "You asked us to use AI. In the last 30 days I shipped <feature or tool> with Claude Code, with tests, and it is in <branch / production / the team's hands>."
2. "I also put a 10-point review checklist on every AI-written PR, so we catch the usual mistakes before they merge."
3. "My estimate is it saves me about <N> hours a week on <the kind of work>. I would like to <next step: roll it out to the team / use it on project X>."

## The 5-line written update (Slack, email, standup notes)

1. What I tried: Claude Code on <repo>, 20 minutes a day for 30 days.
2. What shipped: <feature or internal tool>, <N> commits, <N> tests, link: <url>.
3. What changed in how we work: the AI code review checklist (10 questions) is now part of my PR process; <N> PRs reviewed with it.
4. What it costs: Pro plan, Rs <amount> a month, no extra tooling.
5. What I suggest next: <one concrete ask: a team CLAUDE.md, a 1-hour session for the team, or using it on project X>.

## 3 numbers to bring

| Number | Where it comes from |
|---|---|
| Feature shipped | Name, link to PR or demo, and the date. One is enough. |
| Review checklist adopted | How many PRs went through `cards/review-checklist.md`, and one mistake it caught. |
| Time saved estimate | Hours per week, conservative. Say how you estimated it (for example: "a ticket that used to take me half a day now takes two hours"). |

Do not promise what you have not measured. A small true number beats a big guess.
